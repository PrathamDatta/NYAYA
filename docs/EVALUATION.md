### How lexical is this benchmark?

Mean overlap between a query's content words and its gold passage is **0.935**, and **83%** of queries share ≥95% of their words with the gold passage (headnote clauses largely restate the paragraph they summarise). Overlap is too uniform to split queries into meaningful low/high strata, so no stratified table is shown. This strongly favours keyword matching and is the main reason BM25 looks so good here.

## 4. Evidence gate (when to decline)

Answerable: 120 headnote clauses and 12 hand-written short doctrinal questions (plausibly covered; **not lawyer-verified**). Unrelated: 20 clearly non-legal questions. Coverage = IDF-weighted share of question terms found in the retrieved passage(s); terms unknown to the corpus count against it.

| coverage definition | best threshold | headnote kept | doctrinal kept | unrelated declined | balanced acc. | doctrinal min | unrelated max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| union_top6 | 0.7 | 1.0 | 0.917 | 1.0 | 0.979 | 0.599 | 0.685 |
| union_top3 | 0.6 | 1.0 | 0.833 | 0.95 | 0.933 | 0.41 | 0.667 |
| best_single | 0.45 | 1.0 | 0.917 | 1.0 | 0.979 | 0.374 | 0.421 |

Where `doctrinal min` ≤ `unrelated max` no threshold separates the sets perfectly.

### The rule that ships

Decline unless the **best single passage covers ≥ 0.45** of the question's IDF weight. Measured: headnote questions kept **1.0**, doctrinal kept **0.917**, unrelated declined **1.0**.

Doctrinal questions that are **wrongly declined**: “Does a person living in a reserved forest area have a right to be heard by the Forest Officer before eviction?”

### Why the legal-language detector is advisory, not a gate

Used as a hard condition (CustomInLawBERT score ≥ 0.62) it would have rejected **100%** of the doctrinal questions and 0% of headnote clauses while catching 85% of unrelated ones. It was calibrated on lay narratives, and short doctrinal questions score lower. So it only chooses the wording of a decline.

Caveat: near-domain questions the corpus simply lacks (e.g. tenancy law when no tenancy statute is ingested) are a harder case that these sets do not measure.
